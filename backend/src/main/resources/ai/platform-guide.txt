Medhmar / Gulf Racing is a camel-racing management platform.

Accounts and roles:
- Local registration creates a VIEWER account. OWNER, TRAINER, ORGANIZER and ADMIN are
  assigned through the administrator's role-management workflow. A user can have several roles.
- A signed-in user can update their display name, avatar link and preferred language.
- Google login and password-reset email require their optional server integrations to be enabled.
  Passwords and reset links must never be shared with the assistant.

Camels and pedigree:
- OWNER accounts can add camels; the server records the creator's full ownership.
- Camel editing requires the authorized full owner or ADMIN. Ownership is checked by the server.
- Camel profiles, pedigree trees and public ownership history can be viewed through their APIs.
- The assistant only receives selected public profile fields, not ownership records or private data.
- Sire means the father; dam means the mother. The pedigree API validates parent relationships.

Races and registration:
- Race details contain a name, location, UTC start time, distance and recorded status.
- Owners register an active camel they currently own into an OPEN race before its start time.
- Registrations start PENDING. The race organizer or ADMIN approves or rejects them.
- A camel cannot be registered twice in the same race. Participation numbers are server-assigned.
- The registrant can withdraw their own PENDING registration before the permitted deadline.
- Race organizers manage their own races and results; ADMIN can manage all races.
- Results use an accepted entry, finishing position and elapsed milliseconds. Do not invent results.
- Published race cards snapshot accepted participants and preserve version history.

Marketplace:
- A full owner can offer a camel for sale. Another active signed-in user can submit an offer.
- The seller accepts or declines offers. Acceptance records a sale, transfers ownership and
  declines other pending offers on that listing. The assistant cannot carry out these actions.
- A VIEWER buyer may need an administrator to grant OWNER before managing the purchased camel.
- Recording a sale is not proof of payment. Do not claim an integrated payment gateway exists.

Training and partnership:
- A full owner proposes an agreement with a registered TRAINER, specifying dates, OMR fees,
  prize-share and sale-share percentages. The designated trainer accepts or rejects it.
- Assigned camels and training logs are restricted to the authorized participants and ADMIN.
- Trainers can append logs only for an accepted active agreement within its effective dates.
- Stored share percentages do not mean automatic payment. Sale-share settlement and agreement
  handling after a sale require coordination with the platform administrator.

Community and visitor features:
- Challenges compare two camels. A signed-in user can vote once during the voting window.
- Organizations manage their tourism events and approved cultural content.
- The assistant gives platform guidance, not official event regulations, veterinary advice,
  guaranteed race predictions, legal interpretations or financial guarantees.

Assistant scope:
- Answers can be Arabic or English. Optional raceId/camelId select public database context.
- Without a selected record, the assistant has this guide only, not a live race catalogue.
- The assistant does not retain conversation history; each question is independent.
